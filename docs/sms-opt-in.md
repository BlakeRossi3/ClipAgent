# SMS Opt-in: Sterling personal assistant texts

**Operated by Blake Rossi (sole proprietor).** This page documents how the single recipient of these messages opts in.

## Who receives messages

Only one person: the application's owner, Blake Rossi. No phone numbers are collected from the public and no one else is ever messaged.

## How opt-in works

1. The owner opens his ClipAgent application's **Settings → Your phone** page on his own computer.
2. He enters his own mobile number and saves it.
3. He presses **Send me a test text**, and the application sends this confirmation message:

> Sterling: You're signed up for personal assistant texts (check-ins, reminders, daily summaries). Msg frequency varies, up to 8/day. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

## Sample messages

> Good morning, sir. Yesterday's Shorts drew 1,240 views. One approval awaits you on the dashboard.

> How many hours did you work this week, sir?

> Logged, sir: 38.5 hours, roughly $963 before tax.

## Opting out and help

- Reply **STOP** to stop all messages.
- Reply **HELP** for help.
- Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies, up to 8 per day.
