# Privacy Policy

**ClipAgent / Sterling personal assistant — operated by Blake Rossi (sole proprietor)**

Last updated: November 13, 2026

## What this service is

ClipAgent is a private, personal assistant application run by Blake Rossi on his own computer, for his own use. Its assistant ("Sterling") sends text messages to exactly one recipient: Blake Rossi's own mobile phone number. It does not offer messaging to the public and does not collect phone numbers or personal information from anyone else.

## Information collected

- **Mobile phone number:** the owner's own number, entered by the owner in the application's settings, used only to send him the messages he signed up for.
- **Message content:** texts sent to and from that number (check-ins, reminders, daily summaries and the owner's replies), stored locally on the owner's computer so the assistant can respond and keep a record.

## How it is used

Information is used only to send and respond to the owner's own assistant messages. Messages are delivered through Twilio, which processes them as our SMS provider under its own privacy policy.

## Sharing

**We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.** Information is not shared with anyone except the SMS provider (Twilio) as needed to deliver messages, or where required by law.

## Retention and control

Data is kept on the owner's computer until he deletes it. Reply **STOP** at any time to stop all messages, or **HELP** for help.
